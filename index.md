## Notes

- **Language switching** was performed via `localStorage.setItem('orkod.locale', '<code>')` before each locale batch.
- **RTL (Arabic)** screenshots confirm the layout mirrors correctly — navigation, text alignment, and Body Explorer all flip.
- **Body Explorer** (`/body`) loads a Three.js canvas; captures waited 3s for scene settlement.
- **Home page** (`/home`) includes hero section and feature cards; scrolled captures show below-fold content.
- **All routes** are client-side SPA routes served by Vite dev server through Caddy reverse proxy.
- **Self-signed TLS** on localhost — Playwright configured with `ignoreHTTPSErrors: true`.
