Scope
-----
Follow-up to Round 25 on the running site at https://localhost (Vite dev server,
self-signed cert, headless Edge via playwright-core) and on the production build
served by `vite preview` (service worker enabled/blocked as noted). Two files
changed:

  frontend/src/views/HomePage.vue   (layout + resize correction)
  frontend/vite.config.mts          (three.js chunk split, /models/ SW cache)

No redesign, no copy change, no new dependency, no WebGL context change, no
backend/database/Docker/README/Makefile change, no scroll snapping added. The app
repo was not committed, staged or pushed; only the review artefacts were pushed.

What was wrong
--------------
1) Tablet band 768..960 (non-snap layout). Chapters were content-height, while
   `.orkod-public` keeps `min-height: calc(100svh - chrome)`. At 768x1024 the deck
   ended at y=423 and the footer only started at y=1024 (100svh): a ~361px pale
   #F7FAF5 band between the dark chapter and the footer. 960x800 had 76px, reduced
   motion 1024x768 had 34px, 1280x800 had ~2px.
2) French short phones. At 390x844 the chapter 02 CTA ended at y=838 (5.7px from
   the edge); at 360x800 it ended at y=848, i.e. ~48px below the fold. The long
   French heading/body wraps to extra lines. en/ar fit at both sizes.
3) Same-mode resize drift. 390x844 -> 360x800 preserves the document scrollY while
   chapter 01 reflows from 844 to 800px, so #hp-ch2 ended at top=-44 and the book
   cover at top=24.5: its top 32.5px sat under the 57px fixed header until the next
   scroll. Cross-mode changes were already handled by Round 25.
4) Every page preloaded `vendor` (1,093.92kB raw / 316.48kB gzip) because three.js
   was merged into that shared chunk, even though only the lazy /body route imports
   it. Every visitor downloaded ~670kB of 3D engine on the language gate, home,
   book, donations, etc.
5) /body re-downloaded the 1.1MB body shell + 5.4MB organ bundle + 242KB manifest
   on every visit; the SW had no runtime rule for /models/.

Changes
-------
HomePage.vue (CSS):
- `@media (min-width:768px) .hp-chapters:not(.hp-chapters--snap) .hp-chapter`
  now gets `min-height: calc(100svh - var(--bx-chrome-top,72px))`, `display:flex`,
  `align-items:center`, mirroring the snapped desktop chapter. Existing `.hp-wrap`
  keeps its own `min(100% - 48px, 1240px)` width and inline auto margins.
- Mobile `.hp-chapter--two` gets `min-height: 100svh`. Mobile chapters scroll under
  the fixed header (scroll-margin 0), so minus-chrome was 57px short once the
  trims below made its content shorter than the viewport; this removes the last
  pale strip above the footer.
- Short-phone spacing (chapter 02 only) now applies at `max-height:860px` (was
  830): eyebrow margin 14->9, badge 18->12, body 16->10, actions 32->16, grid gap
  28->20, stage padding 10->6, cover 196->190. A second tier at
  `max-height:810px` trims a further 12px of rhythm (eyebrow 7, badge 10, body 8,
  actions 14, grid gap 16). No type size changes; the CTA stays 52px tall.
HomePage.vue (JS):
- `onViewportResize()` runs on `resize`, updates the chapter indicator as before,
  and for width-only changes (not snap mode) re-aligns the current chapter after a
  220ms debounce, but only when its top is within 1..56px of the viewport top. A
  free-scrolling visitor is never yanked, and height-only resizes (mobile URL bar)
  do nothing. The timer is cleared on unmount.
vite.config.mts:
- manualChunks: `node_modules/three` -> `vendor-three`. It is still precached by
  the PWA (offline /body) but no longer modulepreloaded by other routes.
- workbox runtimeCaching: `/models/` -> CacheFirst, cache `body-models-cache`,
  12 entries / 30 days, statuses 0/200. Comment records that the anatomy bundle is
  not fingerprinted, so a rebuild must bump the cache name.

--------------------

  the seven-organ figure; repeat visits are now cache hits. A smaller first load
  needs an asset-pipeline rebuild/decimation, not a runtime change.
- The `/models/` CacheFirst cache is keyed by URL; anatomy files are not
  fingerprinted. Rebuilding them requires bumping `body-models-cache` (documented
  in vite.config.mts).
- Vitest's 25 failing files are the unchanged backend-only suites; backend on
  :9091 was intentionally not started.
- No visual inspection of the PNGs is claimed. All evidence is DOM geometry, hit
  tests, layout arithmetic, pixel statistics/hashes and browser assertions.
- Round 21/22/23/24/25 preview directories were left untouched.
