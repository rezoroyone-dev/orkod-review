## Verification Summary

- **`/start` page**: Two-panel choice layout (know your condition vs. questionnaire), nature plate background with aurora glow, gold-accented CTAs, works on desktop and mobile, Arabic RTL supported.
- **`/home` page**: Chapter 1↔2 scrolling settles cleanly on chapter boundaries at desktop and mobile. Chapter indicators agree with visible chapter. No stranding between chapters — debounced snap-to-chapter fallback added for environments where `scrollend` doesn't fire reliably.
- **`/body` page**: All 7 organs (brain, heart, lungs, liver, stomach, intestines, kidneys) selectable with highlights, leader-line callouts, and detail panels. Camera frames correctly with larger figure (FRAME_MARGIN 1.24 → figure fills ~81% of stage height). Brighter stage background (#04120B → #05140B), enhanced callout pills, gold secondary buttons. Works on desktop and mobile 390px.
- **Tests**: 25/25 frontend tests pass. Typecheck PASS. Lint PASS.

## Design Changes

**StartJourney.vue:**
- Richer dark background (#0B1F16 → #08180F) with aurora gradient field
- Panel borders with gold hairline (rgba(198,162,74,0.12) → 0.45 on hover)
- Box shadows on panels for depth
- Gold-accented CTA text (#C6A24A → #F2E2B9 on hover)
- Larger titles (font-weight 700, tighter line-height)
- Refined scrim gradients for copy legibility

**HomePage.vue:**
- Page-level aurora gradient field
- Gold active chapter indicator (#DCE8D8 → #C6A24A)
- Gold active numeral color (#F7FAF5 → #F2E2B9)
- Debounced snap-to-chapter fallback in `scheduleSync` rAF callback
- `programmaticScroll` guard prevents `syncChapter`/`snapToChapter` from overwriting `currentChapter` during programmatic navigation
- Direct `scrollTop` assignment in `snapToChapter` (replaces unreliable `scrollTo()` in headless environments)

**BodyExplorer3D.vue:**
- Brighter stage background (#04120B → #05140B)
- Larger figure (FRAME_MARGIN 1.34 → 1.24, figure fills ~81% of stage height)
- Enhanced stage glow (opacity 0.6→1.0, scale 1→1.05, duration 5s→6s)
- Larger callout pills with stronger shadows
- Gold secondary button (#C6A24A with font-weight 700)
- Prominent organ selector active states
- Page-level aurora background field

**HumanBody3D.vue:**
- FRAME_MARGIN 1.34 → 1.24 (larger figure)
