Round 24 - mobile book cover cropping (360x800) in homepage chapter 02
Generated 2026-10-22T13:20:00Z

What was wrong
--------------
Round 23 trimmed the top of chapter 02 on short phones to win room for the CTA.
At 360x800 that set padding-top to 24px, and the 3D book then started at y=40
while the header occupies y=0..57 (sticky, 57px tall on phones). The top 16.9px
of the cover - the entire top edge, including the bevel - sat behind the header.
Hit-testing the top centre of the cover returned the header's "Start your
journey" button, not the book. At 390x844 the base rule (padding-top 75.96px)
put the cover at y=102, so only 360-class short phones were affected.

What changed (frontend/src/views/HomePage.vue only)
---------------------------------------------------
Inside @media (max-width: 767px) and (max-height: 830px):
  .hp-chapter--two            padding-top: calc(var(--bx-chrome-top, 57px) + 7px)
                              (64px at the current 57px header)
  .hp-chapter--two .hp-book-stage   padding-block: 10px   (was 18px)
  .hp-chapter--two .hp-book-cover   width: 196px          (was the 210px floor)

The section now starts just below the header, the stage keeps a thin frame of
air, and the cover is 6.7% smaller on these short viewports so the CTA keeps its
bottom gap. The book art, cloth texture, bevel, gold print, spine/pages, page
backdrop and forest grading are untouched; only the rendered size changes. The
390x844 rule set is not touched at all.

Resize drift (pre-existing, now smaller)
----------------------------------------
Changing the viewport height keeps the document scroll offset, so a snapped
section drifts by the height delta until the next scroll re-snaps. At 360x800,
shrinking to 780px without re-snapping leaves the cover 4.6px behind the header;
with the Round-23 CSS the same sequence left it 36.9px behind. Indicator stays
02/02 throughout, and the next snap restores the 15.4px clearance. No scroll
re-snap logic was added (out of scope, and it risks jumps when mobile browser
chrome shows/hides).
