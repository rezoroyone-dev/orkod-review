Branch `visual-review/nature-green-round-21`, based on `main` @ `bdcd0bf`.
All captures come from the running dev server (`https://localhost`) in headless Edge;
this folder also carries `verification-report.txt`, generated by

## What Round 21 changed

**1. `/start` whole-body preview is legible over the forest.** The render was re-cut
with a denser shell (alpha 0.42 -> 0.52) and a fresnel rim (`uRimStrength` 0.85 ->
1.15, new `uRimAlpha` 0.46), and the page stopped dimming it: opacity 0.62 -> 0.86
(hover 0.95 -> 1.0), a contact + ambient shadow, and a local radial shade pool
behind the figure. Measured on the same asset, same viewport, same mask
(alpha > 0.25), the figure's contrast against the forest it covers:

| band | median abs dL before | after | pixels with dL >= 10 before | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| head | 51.7 | **81.7** | 96.4% | **98.2%** |
| torso + arms | 25.3 | **47.7** | 77.8% | **89.4%** |
| thighs | 23.7 | **42.0** | 88.4% | **98.7%** |
| legs + feet | 27.3 | **45.0** | 93.5% | **99.1%** |
| whole figure | 25.7 | **45.0** | 85.0% | **94.5%** |

Asset alpha coverage rose from a flat 0.42 shell to: head 67% of pixels >= 0.60
(28% >= 0.90), torso 54%, thighs 27%, legs+feet 28% (was 0%), so the limbs are no
longer a veil. The figure is trimmed to its own alpha box (900x1461 desktop,
560x900 mobile) and renders 446 px tall = 50% of the 714x900 panel.

**2. The brain reads in the cranium, not across the face.** The organ was already
correctly placed and scaled (containment verification: brain y 1.547-1.701 m inside
1.5-1.73 m; 1 of 401 sampled vertices outside the shell within tolerance). The defect
was compositing: the shell reveal window was centred on the brain's bounding-box
centre (y 1.6256 m, the eye line) and the shell was only 0.36 opaque. Round 21
narrows the window (`DEEP_REVEAL_RADIUS` 0.13 -> 0.105), centres it on the brain's
upper-mass core (y 1.66709 m), strengthens it (0.18 -> 0.5) and adds a face guard
(alpha 0.94 below 1.60 m with a 16 mm ramp). Layer-isolated frames at the focus
camera, head window x 348-527:

| band (874x724 canvas) | brain contribution px > 8 | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| crown -> brow | | 9,912 | **9,821** (vault reveal intact) |
| brow -> eyes | | 1,753 | **1,474** (the 16 mm guard ramp) |
| eyes -> chin | | 2,190 | **0** (brain no longer over the face) |

The face below the eyes measures median luminance 162 both with and without the
organ layer (a solid pearl head), while the vault gains 9.8k px of the organ's own
light. The callout anchor is unchanged at (693, 426) page / (435, 270) canvas.

**3. Book claims audited against the project's own content.** Removed the three
unverifiable bullet claims ("Healing with energy and sharpened senses", "Deep
breathing exercises for the stress response", "Energy bathing and restoring your
balance") from all four locales, and replaced the cover sub-line, cover foot-line,
page subtitle and "About the book" paragraph with statements about the book's
preparation status only ("A book in preparation - not yet published", "no contents,
format or price are settled yet"). The honest status ribbon, the concept-cover label,
the concept note, one book, no price, no checkout and the real booking CTA are kept.

**4. Arabic RTL** keeps the 3D model physically correct: with the same organ and
camera, English and Arabic canvases differ by a mean of 0.76/255 with bright-mass
centroids at x 440.7 vs 440.3, and the callout anchor matches within 1 px. The canvas
itself has no CSS transform in RTL.

## Limitations

- No visual inspection was performed or is claimed; the images accompany the numbers
  but were not judged by eye.
- The ~4 mm occipital protrusion of the brain at y 1.60-1.63 m (back of the head,
  invisible from the front) is within the fit tolerance and was deliberately not
  rescaled or moved.
- Backend-dependent suites cannot run without the Go service; they are excluded, not
  reported as green.
